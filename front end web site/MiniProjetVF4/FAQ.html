Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

For Sellers

How do I list my car for sale?

To list your car for sale, go to the "Sell Your Car" page and fill out the form with your car details.

What information do I need to provide when selling my car?

You need to provide details such as make, model, year, price, and photos of your car.

How long does it take to sell my car?

The time it takes to sell your car varies depending on market demand and the condition of your car.

Can I edit my car listing after it's been posted?

Yes, you can edit your car listing by logging into your account and accessing the listing management section.

How do I communicate with potential buyers?

You can communicate with potential buyers through the contact information provided on your car listing.

Is there a fee for listing my car?

No, listing your car for sale on our platform is free of charge.

For Buyers

How do I search for cars on the platform?

You can use the search bar on the homepage to search for cars by make, model, year, and other criteria.

Can I schedule a test drive for a car I'm interested in?

Yes, you can contact the seller directly through the contact information provided on the car listing to schedule a test drive.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment methods vary depending on the seller. You can discuss payment options with the seller directly.

Can I negotiate the price of a car?

Yes, you can negotiate the price with the seller. Contact the seller directly to discuss pricing.

Are there any hidden fees when buying a car?

No, there are no hidden fees. The price listed on the car listing is the final price unless otherwise specified by the seller.

How do I report a suspicious listing?

If you come across a suspicious listing, please contact us through the contact page with details of the listing.
